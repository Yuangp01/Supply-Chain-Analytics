
# Supply Chain & E-commerce Analytics Dashboard

### Power BI | Supply Chain Analytics | Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze supply chain operations, sales performance, product profitability, and shipping efficiency.

The project transforms transactional data into actionable insights to help identify delivery challenges, monitor operational performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | $2.25M |
| Total Profit | $248.63K |
| Total Orders | 17,350 |
| Customers | 9,300 |
| Late Delivery Rate | 57.2% |
| Average Shipping Days | 3.5 |

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

- Monthly sales trends
- Revenue and profit performance
- Top 5 categories by profit
- Customer and order analytics
- Order-level performance investigations

### 2. Delivery & Profitability

- Delivery performance monitoring
- Shipping efficiency analysis
- Profitability insights
- Operational bottleneck identification

## Business Questions

This dashboard helps explore:

1. Which product categories generate the highest profit?
2. How do sales change throughout the year?
3. What percentage of deliveries arrive late?
4. How does shipping performance vary across regions?
5. Which orders and products require further investigation?

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI: Interactive dashboards and reporting
- Data Modeling: Data relationships and analytical structure
- Data Visualization: KPI cards, charts, tables, and slicers
- Business Intelligence: Performance monitoring and operational analysis

## Key Findings

- The dashboard reports $2.25M in total sales.
- Total profit reaches approximately $248.63K.
